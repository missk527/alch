ALC8. Solution Volume and Concentration

Key Notes (Synced with Video)

Concentration | Core Concept
  • Concentration measures how much solute is dissolved in a given volume of solution.
  • A concentrated solution contains a large amount of solute per unit volume.
  • A dilute solution contains a small amount of solute per unit volume.
Core Equation | Moles, Concentration, and Volume
  • The central equation is n = c × V.
  • n is the amount in moles (mol), c is concentration in mol dm−3, and V is volume in dm3.
  • Rearrangements are c = n ÷ V and V = n ÷ c.
  • Always convert the volume to dm3 before using these equations.
Volume Conversion | cm³ and dm³
  • 1 dm3 equals 1000 cm3.
  • Convert cm3 to dm3 by dividing by 1000.
  • Convert dm3 to cm3 by multiplying by 1000.
  • For example, 100 cm3 = 0.100 dm3 and 25.0 cm3 = 0.0250 dm3.
Worked Example | Calculating Moles
  • For 10 dm3 of a 2 mol dm−3 solution, use n = cV.
  • n = 2 × 10 = 20 mol.
  • The concentration and volume units cancel correctly to leave moles.
Mixing Solutions | Potassium-Ion Concentration
  • 100 cm3 of 0.1 mol dm−3 KCl contains 0.010 mol KCl and therefore 0.010 mol K+.
  • 100 cm3 of 0.1 mol dm−3 K2CO3 contains 0.010 mol K2CO3 and therefore 0.020 mol K+.
  • Total K+ = 0.030 mol and total volume = 0.200 dm3.
  • [K+] = 0.030 ÷ 0.200 = 0.15 mol dm−3.
Citric Acid | Small-Volume Calculation
  • Convert 25.0 cm3 to 0.0250 dm3.
  • For a 0.010 mol dm−3 solution, n = 0.010 × 0.0250.
  • The amount of citric acid is 2.50 × 10−4 mol.
Stoichiometry | H₃PO₄ and NaOH
  • 30 cm3 of 0.1 mol dm−3 H3PO4 contains 0.003 mol of acid.
  • One mole of H3PO4 reacts with three moles of NaOH, so 0.009 mol NaOH is required.
  • At 0.1 mol dm−3, V = n ÷ c = 0.009 ÷ 0.1 = 0.09 dm3.
  • 0.09 dm3 equals 90 cm3.
Mass to Concentration | Magnesium Chloride
  • First calculate amount using n = m ÷ M.
  • Mr(MgCl2) = 24.3 + (2 × 35.5) = 95.3 g mol−1.
  • 6.00 g MgCl2 contains approximately 0.0630 mol MgCl2.
  • Each formula unit produces two Cl− ions, giving 0.126 mol Cl−.
  • In 0.100 dm3, [Cl−] = 0.126 ÷ 0.100 = 1.26 mol dm−3.
Exam Method | Reliable Calculation Steps
  • Identify the required quantity and write the relevant equation.
  • Convert volumes into dm3 and masses into moles where necessary.
  • Use formula subscripts or balanced-equation ratios to find the required species' amount.
  • Substitute values with units, calculate carefully, and give an appropriately rounded final answer.
